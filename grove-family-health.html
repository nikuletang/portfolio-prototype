01 Project overview

A modern website for a different kind of primary care

Grove Family Health offers a more personal, accessible model of care to individuals and to employers. The practice needed a site that communicated that value and helped each audience understand their options and take the next step.

03 Understanding the challenge

A practice with strong value that wasn't fully reflected online

Grove Family Health delivers a more personal, accessible care model for individuals and employers. Their previous website didn't clearly communicate that value, which made it harder for visitors to understand their options or move forward with confidence.

Where visitors were getting stuck

Patients and employers were visiting the site but didn't always have a clear, guided path to the information they needed. These are the project challenges and design observations that shaped the redesign.

  1. No guided path for each audience

    Patients and employers arrived with different questions, but the site didn't lead either group clearly to the information meant for them.

    give each audience a clear starting point

  2. Services were hard to take in quickly

    Services were grouped in a way that made it harder to quickly understand the available options.

    give services their own, focused pages

  3. The care model needed explaining

    Direct primary care works differently from the insurance-based care many visitors know, and the site needed to explain it more clearly.

    explain the model in plain, reassuring language

  4. No defined journey to a decision

    Both individuals and employers could benefit from a more defined journey toward evaluating plans or enrolling in membership.

    make the next step visible and easy to take

These observations come from the project brief and the design work. They aren't findings from formal user research.

04 Site architecture

Organizing the site for two audiences

I created a sitemap to plan how patients and employers would each find the information that applies to them, and how every page would lead to a next step.

What the structure does

  • Dedicated service pagesServices are broken into their own pages, to make each option easier to understand and easier to find in search.
  • A path for non-membersA separate pathway leads people who aren't yet members step by step toward enrollment.
  • Simpler navigationFewer, clearer choices, so the next step feels obvious from anywhere on the site.

07 High-fidelity design

Key design decisions

In the high-fidelity designs, I brought the structure and visual direction together around three goals. Each decision below shows the challenge, my design response, and why it mattered.

"Why it mattered" describes what each change was intended to do. Measured results are reported separately under Outcomes.

01Helping patients and employers find what's relevant to them

  1. The challengePatients and employers came to the site with different questions, without a clear path to the information meant for them.
  2. Design responseSeparate paths for patients and employers, supported by simplified navigation.Add specifics: how each audience is directed (navigation labels, homepage sections).
  3. Why it matteredEach visitor can get to what applies to them with less effort.Intended benefit

02Making the care model and service options easier to understand

  1. The challengeServices were grouped in a way that made options hard to take in quickly, and the direct primary care model needed a clearer explanation.
  2. Design responseDedicated pages for services, with reassuring, plain-language content about how care works.Add specifics: how the care model is explained (sections, FAQs, comparisons).
  3. Why it matteredVisitors can understand their options before they decide, and each service has its own page, which improves clarity and search visibility.Intended benefit

03Making the next step clear

  1. The challengeVisitors who were ready to act didn't have a clearly defined journey toward enrolling in membership or evaluating a plan.
  2. Design responseA step-by-step pathway for non-members toward enrollment, and visible calls to action throughout the site.Add specifics: where the calls to action sit and what they say.
  3. Why it matteredEvery path ends in an obvious next step, so no one is left wondering what to do next.Intended benefit
